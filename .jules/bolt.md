## 2024-05-18 - Missing DB Indices for Frequently Queried Fields
**Learning:** The database queries for sales history and reporting rely heavily on `sale_date` filtering, and the queries for fetching items/payments per sale rely on `sale_id`. Since SQLite doesn't automatically index foreign keys or frequently queried timestamps unless specified, performance drops linearly with data volume (O(N) full table scan). This application stores every single item transaction in `SaleItems`, making `sale_id` lookup on large databases a major bottleneck.
**Action:** When adding or auditing reporting or transactional features, check if the SQL queries perform range scans (e.g. `BETWEEN ? AND ?`) or joins without indices on those specific columns. Specifically, ensure foreign keys like `sale_id` are explicitly indexed in `schema.sql`.
